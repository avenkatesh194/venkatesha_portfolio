Services

Software consulting when you need judgment before more code.

Consulting is for teams that need a senior view of the problem: what to build, what to leave, how to sequence it, and whether the current product can carry the next idea. I also stay on to help the build if you want the same person in both roles.

Included

What the engagement covers

  • A discovery pass that names users, constraints, and the real risk
  • A technical approach written in language the decision-makers can use
  • A review of an existing website, application, or codebase
  • A delivery sequence with scope that can survive contact with the calendar
  • Office hours with the people who will build or buy the work
A good fit

When clients ask for this

  • A build that has started to sprawl
  • A vendor proposal you want a second reading on
  • A product decision with expensive consequences
  • A team that has developers and needs direction

Engagement

How it usually runs

Read the situation

I look at the product, the constraint, and what has already been tried.

Recommend

You get a written point of view: options, tradeoffs, and a suggested path.

Stay or hand off

I can remain as the builder, or leave a plan your team can run.
