What is the typical project timeline?

A website is often a few weeks once the scope is agreed. A web application, mobile app, or custom system takes longer. The timeline is written before build work starts.

Do you stay after launch?

Yes, when the engagement includes a handover and a short support window. Longer maintenance is a separate agreement.

What do you build with?

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React.js, Node.js, React Native, Android, iOS, SQL, MongoDB, and AWS. The stack follows the product.

How are fees handled?

The fee and the payment schedule are written in the scope. Build work does not start before that scope is agreed.
