Website development · Anonymized example

A retail website the merchandising team can publish to.

The public site had fallen behind the catalog. Product updates waited on a developer, and the pages were difficult to use on a phone. The engagement rebuilt the website around the way the team actually sells.

Website development Retail Remote engagement

Situation

Campaigns and product stories lived in email threads. The website could not keep up, and shoppers on a phone got a squeezed version of the desktop page.

Approach

I mapped the pages a shopper needs, separated campaign content from the catalog, and designed a responsive layout before the build. Search titles and headings were written with the pages, not added at the end.

What shipped

A responsive marketing and catalog website, an editing model the team can follow, and a handover that shows how to publish a product, a story, and a landing page.

Outcome

The team can update the site on its own schedule. Shoppers get the same structure on a phone as on a desktop, and the pages explain the offer without a developer in the loop.
