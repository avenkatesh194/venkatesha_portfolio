Software consulting · Anonymized example

Consulting for a product that needed a path, not another rewrite slogan.

The team knew the product had aged. A full rewrite was the loud option and a risky one. The engagement was software consulting: read the system, name what had to change, and sequence the work so shipping did not stop.

Software consulting Product team Remote engagement

Situation

New features were slow because old decisions were undocumented. Proposals for a rewrite did not say what customers would get first.

Approach

I reviewed the product with the people who change it, separated risks that block delivery from cleanup that can wait, and wrote a sequence the team could fund.

What shipped

A written point of view: what to keep, what to replace, the first release that earns the right to continue, and the risks of doing it in the other order.

Outcome

The team had a plan they could argue with and then follow. The next build started from a shared sequence instead of a slogan.
